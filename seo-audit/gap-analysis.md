## 4. Trust & conversion gaps
1. No proof: reviews, logos, case numbers, badges, team, address. (All need your real info — see TODO list.)
2. Unverified stats already live (150+ projects, 98%, 5.0 rating, 100K+ leads, 150% revenue, Top Rated Agency, 24/7) — competitors also inflate (sharpweb "28k clients"), but this is a trust and legal risk. **Recommendation: replace with verifiable facts.**
3. Form doesn't reliably capture leads.
4. No business hours, no response-time promise backed by process.
